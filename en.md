# Kif-Kif Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 18 October 2026**
**Applies to the Kif-Kif Android app (Google Play, id `com.youkar.equilibre`).**
**Publisher: Youkar.** Contact: YoukarC@hotmail.com

Languages: [Français](./) · [Nederlands](nl.html) · English

> This text accurately describes what the app does. It is not legal advice.

## In one sentence

Kif-Kif works without an account and without a server: what you note in it
stays on your phone, and the publisher receives none of it.

## What stays on your device

Everything you enter or create while using the app: the first names (or team
names) you type in, an emoji and a color for each person, the rituals and games
you create (their free-form names and settings), the history of your moments
(date, time, value and, if you write one, a note), games and their scores,
and your settings (vibration, reduced motion, play posture).

The app also creates a random device identifier, generated on your phone. It is
used only to put your moments in order and to merge a backup; it is never sent
to anyone.

All of it is stored in the app's private space, which other apps cannot access.
Uninstalling Kif-Kif erases this data.

## What leaves your device

Kif-Kif itself makes no network calls: the app does not send any data to the
publisher or to third parties on its own. A few services of your phone or of
Google may still come into play, at your initiative or according to your
settings.

### Kif-Kif+ purchase (Google Play)

Kif-Kif+ is a one-time purchase, handled entirely by **Google Play** (Google
Play Billing library). The app sees neither your payment method nor your
identity; it only keeps an "offer unlocked" flag on the device. To show the
price, carry out the purchase and find a purchase you already made (including
when the app starts, without you buying anything), Google's billing library may
communicate with Google's servers, and it adds its own operational data. This is
why the Internet permission appears in the app. These exchanges are covered by
Google and its privacy policy:
https://policies.google.com/privacy

### Android backup (Google)

If you have turned on your phone's backup, Android may back up Kif-Kif's data
to your Google account and restore it on a new phone. Kif-Kif requires that
this happen **only if that backup is encrypted**. It is a Google service, which
you set (or turn off) in your phone's settings.

### Files you create yourself

In Settings → Your data, you can create a backup: a ZIP file, encrypted if you
choose a passphrase, **unencrypted otherwise**. With Kif-Kif+, you can also
export the history as CSV (readable as is). These files are handed to the
system share sheet: **you** choose the destination (folder, messaging app,
cloud...). Once the file is sent elsewhere, it falls under the policy of the
service you chose. Restoring a backup means merging a file you have chosen.

## Widget and notification

- **Home screen widget**: the app prepares what it displays (ritual title,
  whose turn it is, first names, values) in local storage of the app, which only
  your phone's launcher reads to display it.
- **Notification**: Kif-Kif shows only one, while a timer is running. It is
  local, and permission is requested at the first timer.

## Advertising

No ads, no advertising ID.

## Permissions, and why

- **Google Play billing** — buying Kif-Kif+.
- **Internet** — declared by Google's billing library, for the purchase and
  its exchanges with Google. The app makes no other use of it.
- **Notifications** — showing the running timer. Optional: the app works
  without it.
- **Wake and device startup** — permissions of the Android scheduler that the
  widget uses to stay up to date.

Kif-Kif asks for **no access** to your camera, microphone, location, contacts
or your phone's shared storage.

## What the app does not do

There is **no account** to create: no email address, no password.

There is **no audience measurement**, no analytics tool, no crash report sent
remotely, no publisher telemetry, and no third-party component observing how
you use it.

Nothing you note is sold, rented or passed on by the publisher: it receives no
copy of it.

## Closed-test builds

Only in closed-test builds, a "Share my usage numbers" button produces an
anonymous summary: the template of each ritual, the number of people, the
chosen visual and the number of moments per week. It contains no first name,
no name you typed, no note and no time. The tester sends it **themselves**, if
they wish. This button does not exist in the published version.

## Children

Kif-Kif is intended for people aged 18 and over (couples' use) and is not aimed
at children.

## Your rights

The GDPR gives you rights of access, rectification, erasure and portability.
The publisher holds none of your personal data: it is on your phone, and you
stay in control.

- **Access and rectification**: everything is displayed and editable in the
  app.
- **Portability**: the backup and the CSV export (Kif-Kif+) produce files you
  can take with you.
- **Erasure**: deleting an item in the app removes it from view (it stays in
  the app's database, on the phone, so it can be restored); uninstall the app
  to erase everything from the device. If you turned on Android backup, you manage that copy in
  your phone's and Google account's settings.
